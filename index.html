Licensing, in plain terms

Four situations cover most orders. Open the one that matches yours.

Covers installing a face in design and word-processing apps on your own machines. Priced per person, not per machine, so a laptop-and-desktop setup is fine. Seats: named users · Files: unlimited

Covers self-hosting or serving through a font CDN for a specific domain. Priced in monthly-pageview tiers that you can move between as traffic changes. Domains: 1 primary + staging · Formats: woff2, woff

Covers bundling font files inside a shipped desktop or mobile app, where the type isn't user-editable or extractable. Installs: tiered by volume · Reseller terms: on request

Covers on-air, film, and large-format physical display use, including motion-rendered type. Cleared for a named production or an ongoing broadcast identity. Territory: worldwide · Term: perpetual for the named use
